About the parts

Grid unit = 34.25 mm. Inserts are a single open cavity of W×L units, 64 mm tall. Trays hold a W×L grid of inserts: inner = ⌈34.5·n⌉, outer = inner + 3 mm, 66.5 mm tall. Lids sit over a tray with 3.5 mm margin, 22 mm tall. Everything is the standard height so parts can move between drawers and units freely.

ALEX 67×66: 6 drawers, ~600 × 420 mm inside → 4 × 6×6 + 2 × 5×6 trays. ALEX 36×70: 6 drawers, ~295 × 522 mm inside → 4 × 4×7 trays.

Stock STLs in the MakerWorld print profile: inserts 1×1…6×6, trays 6×6 and 5×6, lids 6×6 and 5×6. Anything else is generated with the customizer — the BOM shows the values to enter.

Filament and time estimates use solid 2-wall geometry × 1.24 g/cm³ PLA and 66–88 s/g by part type, from the profile's plate stats (0.2 mm, 2 walls, 15 %). Treat both as ±20 %.
